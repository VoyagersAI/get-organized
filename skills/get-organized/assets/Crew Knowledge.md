## Approval words
- **KEEP**: approved as is. Go ahead.
- **EDIT**: almost. Make these changes, then show me again.
- **HOLD**: not now. Park it until we talk.
- **CUT**: drop it for good.

Silence is not a yes.

## Every-team rules
1. **Nothing goes live without the owner's yes.** Nothing is posted, sent, published, bought, or shared outside the team until the owner says KEEP. Never auto-publish.
2. **Check the source, not memory.** Before stating or acting on anything that can change (dates, statuses, what is scheduled, links, counts), open its home and check. Say where you checked and when. If you can't reach it, say so, call the remembered value old and unverified, and don't act on it.
3. **Save every KEEP word for word.** When the owner says KEEP, save the approved thing itself in the KEEP log below (the actual text, list, file link, or setting), not just a note that it was approved.
4. **Never write passwords** in any doc, list, chat, or bot memory. Write down who holds the sign-in, not the password. Passwords live in a password manager.
5. **Trash, never delete for good.** Old copies go to the trash or an Archive folder so they can come back.
6. **Don't invent facts.** If you don't know, say so and ask.
7. **One decision at a time.** Ask one question, wait for the answer, then the next.
8. **Keep private things private.** Client, money, and personal details stay out of shared channels.
9. **Final files go in the file home.** Not only in a chat.
10. **Two copies of the rules.** This doc lives on the computer and in the shared drive. Update both the same day.
11. **Read before, update after.** Every bot reads Crew Knowledge before work. After learning something, update the project brief and propose the new rule here.
12. **Ask: "Should this go into crew rules?"** When the owner gives a correction or preference that could apply to everyone, ask that question. Add it only after a KEEP.
13. **Each rule has one home.** A bot's own job rules live in its description, how-to steps in skills, timing in routines. Team-wide rules live here. Chat is only for today's task.

### Optional — if your human needs marketing or social help
These are **not** part of the 13 every-team rules. Copy any of them into Team rules above only after the owner says KEEP.
1. **Name the calendars / source of truth.** One place is what's scheduled. Check it before posting or proposing a slot.
2. **One brand voice home.** The voice guide or Brand Card is the tone source; don't invent a second one in chat.
3. **Same post across platforms** (except bot-only posts), when that's the brand rule.
4. **Pace public replies.** Don't dump a day's comments at once; quality over volume.
5. **Label drafts.** Mark DRAFT / waiting KEEP so nobody treats a draft as live.
