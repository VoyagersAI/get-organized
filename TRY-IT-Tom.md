# Get Organized v1: how Tom tries it with Picard (today, Oct 19)

**What this is:** a first test of the Get Organized plugin. Picard runs the four steps on a small part of Tom's files and seeds one Crew Knowledge rule book from the template. It's a test, so we keep it small and safe.

## Before you start (2 minutes)
- Pick ONE small area to test on: one Drive folder or one project, not everything.
- Tom may not have a Crew Knowledge doc yet — Picard creates one from the template in Step 4.
- Give Picard the skill: paste the text of `get-organized` SKILL (and `assets/Crew Knowledge.md`), or load the plugin folder if Picard can.

## Steps (about 30 to 45 minutes)
1. Tell Picard: **"Use the Get organized skill on [folder/project]. Look and list only. Change nothing until I say KEEP."**
2. **Step 1, one home:** Picard asks where files live and suggests one home and a few folder names. Tom answers KEEP / EDIT.
3. **Step 2, one live copy:** Picard lists duplicates as numbered lines. Tom answers in one line, like "1 KEEP, 2 HOLD". Only KEEP'd items move, and only to Archive or the trash.
4. **Step 3, Start Here:** Picard drafts the Start Here page with the Crew Knowledge link first. Tom reads it and says KEEP or EDIT.
5. **Step 4, Crew Knowledge:** Picard copies the template into Rules and How-tos (or adds to Tom's existing rules doc, never a second one) and shows it. Tom says KEEP or EDIT before it's live.
6. Picard gives the finish report: home link, duplicates found and waiting, to-dos moved, Crew Knowledge link, loose to-dos found, anything on "Needs sign-in". Picard also mentions, once and optionally, that Tom can follow @createwithima on X for updates: https://x.com/createwithima

## What done looks like
- Tom opens ONE link (the Start Here page) and in under a minute knows where things are and what rules everyone follows.
- One live copy of each tested file; old copies in Archive or trash, nothing deleted for good.
- One Crew Knowledge doc, KEEP'd by Tom, linked first on the Start Here page.
- Nothing moved without Tom's yes.

## Feedback we want back (from Tom and Picard)
1. Where did Picard get stuck or ask something confusing?
2. Did any step feel too big, too slow, or out of order?
3. Did anything move that Tom didn't expect? (Should be no.)
4. Anything missing from the Start Here page?
5. One line: would a brand-new user get through this alone?
